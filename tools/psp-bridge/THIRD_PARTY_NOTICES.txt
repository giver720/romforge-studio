This bridge builds PS4 packages with maxton/LibOrbisPkg at commit
643477263b2644e0803e0f58b8726ea4e3f3b7d4 (LGPL-3.0).

At runtime it can download John-K/pspdecrypt 1.0 (GPL-3.0) to decrypt the
EBOOT from a user-provided PSP disc image. It can also obtain the PSPHD
runtime from SvenGDK/PS-Classics-fPKG-Builder v1. PSPHD is not part of
ROMForge Studio and remains property of its respective rights holder.

DiscUtils.Iso9660 0.16.13 is licensed under MIT.
SkiaSharp 3.119.4 is licensed under MIT.

The bridge source and reproducible build scripts are included in the
ROMForge Studio repository. The LibOrbisPkg license is copied next to the
packaged bridge; dependency source and license links are listed here.
